SMART HOME

Miniature Technology Smart Home System — an IoT-based educational prototype featuring automated lighting, security gates, and door control integrated with ESP32 and Google Home.

This SMART HOME system was a professional commission from SMAN 2 Bekasi, executed by the Robotik SMKN 1 Kota Bekasi organization which I founded. As the Project Manager, I led a 5-person team through a 3-month development cycle, overseeing everything from initial client requirements gathering to the final delivery of a fully functional IoT educational prototype.

Challenge

Developing a comprehensive smart home miniaturization that is both cost-effective and highly functional for educational purposes. We needed to create robust mechanical systems for gates and doors using limited resources. A key challenge was repurposing electronic waste, specifically modifying internal DVD player motors to act as precise gate actuators, requiring custom mechanical adjustments and electrical isolation via relays.

What I built

As the Project Manager and Organization Founder, I didn't just build a device; I built the workflow and team structure required to deliver a professional product. I managed the 3-month timeline, coordinated between the hardware and software sub-teams, and ensured that our technical implementation—from ESP32 logic to the mechanical gate systems—strictly aligned with the client's educational requirements.

Impact

The project successfully transitioned our school organization from a hobbyist club to a productive tech entity capable of handling real-world clients. Delivering this system to SMAN 2 Bekasi provided them with a high-quality educational tool while establishing Robotik SMKN 1's reputation for excellence. It proved that student-led organizations can contribute meaningful, functional technology to the community.

What I learned

I learned to bridge the gap between a client's high-level vision and the concrete technical steps required to bring it to life. Beyond the engineering work, this project taught me how to lead a team through the pressure of a three-month deadline, manage project resources, and maintain clear, consistent communication between technical teams and those without a technical background.
