ECOTRA

Ecosystem Optimization for Chili Cultivation Through AI and IoT Automation — an intelligent precision-farming prototype that detects crop disease and automates pesticide spraying using CNN and IoT.

ECOTRA is a GROUP PROJECT — a PKM-KC (Student Creativity Program) proposal developed by a 5-person multidisciplinary team at Gunadarma University. As the AI Engineer, my responsibility was to develop and deploy the machine learning model (CNN MobileNetV2) for chili leaf disease detection, and integrate the Google Gemini LLM as an interactive chatbot assistant within the mobile application.

Challenge

Chili crops in Indonesia are highly vulnerable to pest and disease outbreaks — antraknosa, Thrips, and leaf-curl virus can destroy up to 100% of a field if not detected early. Existing IoT systems only provided rule-based alerts without visual intelligence. Farmers still relied on "blanket spraying" which wastes pesticide and harms the ecosystem. There was a clear need for a system that could see, decide, and act autonomously.

What I built

As the AI Engineer on this 5-person group project, I was responsible for two AI pillars: (1) training a CNN (MobileNetV2) model using Python and TensorFlow to classify chili leaf diseases from camera input in real-time on an edge device (Orange Pi Zero 3), and (2) integrating the Google Gemini LLM API into the React Native mobile app to serve as an interactive agricultural chatbot — translating raw sensor data into plain-language recommendations for farmers.

Impact

The ECOTRA proposal advances the concept of precision agriculture: instead of spraying the entire field, the system triggers a targeted spot-spray only when disease is detected on a specific plant. This approach has the potential to significantly cut pesticide usage and prevent crop failure. The proposal also directly supports Indonesia's national food sovereignty agenda, which is the central theme for this PKM-KC cycle.

What I learned

Working in a 5-person cross-disciplinary team — spanning IoT, AI, mobile development, UI/UX, and administration — taught me the importance of clear role boundaries and inter-domain communication. I learned how to bridge AI research with embedded hardware constraints, and how to frame a technical solution around a real societal problem to craft a compelling, fundable research proposal. Identifying the right problem is just as important as building the right solution.
